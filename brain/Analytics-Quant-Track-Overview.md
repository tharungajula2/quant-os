# Analytics & Quant Track

> This system is the quantitative core of the tharungajula portfolio. It is designed to demonstrate high-fidelity technical depth in credit risk modeling, financial engineering, and advanced data science.
>
> Use this explorer to navigate through the first-principles foundations and applied masterclasses that define this technical track.

---

## The Analytical Arsenal

This track is built at the intersection of **quantitative modeling**, **risk governance**, and **automated data intelligence**. It is structured to show how raw complexity is transformed into clinical insight through three distinct layers:

1.  **Risk & Banking Context**: Deep-dives into lending portfolios, default risk, loss estimation, and regulatory provisioning logic.
2.  **Quantitative Modeling**: Advanced application of Python, SQL, and statistical learning to convert messy data into predictive power.
3.  **Governance & Implementation**: Rigorous documentation, validation workflows, and model-monitoring systems designed for high-stakes environments.

---

## Technical Capabilities

The track showcases a unified skill set applied to complex financial and behavioral datasets:

- **Mathematical Modeling**: Linear/Logistic regression, Scorecard development, Neural Networks, and Tree-based ensembles (XGBoost, Random Forests).
- **Validation Frameworks**: Deep understanding of metrics like KS, AUROC, Gini, PSI, and stability diagnostics.
- **Data Engineering**: Fluent use of Python/SQL for high-performance extraction, cleaning, and feature engineering.
- **NLP & AI**: Sentiment analysis, text-feature extraction, and unstructured data processing for alternative signal generation.
